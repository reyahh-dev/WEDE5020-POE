Our Services

Rea's Bakery provides a variety of baking services for everyday customers, celebrations and special events.

Custom Cakes

We create customised cakes for birthdays, anniversaries, graduations and other special occasions.

Customers can discuss their preferred cake flavour, design, size and theme with our bakery team.

Wedding Cakes

Rea's Bakery provides personalised wedding cakes designed to suit the theme and style of your special day.

Customers can enquire about different flavours, sizes, designs and decoration options.

Event Catering

Our bakery provides baked goods for meetings, parties, celebrations and other events.

Catering options can include cupcakes, pastries, muffins and other baked products depending on the requirements of the event.

Bulk Orders

Customers and businesses can place bulk orders for bread, pastries, cupcakes and other bakery products.

Bulk orders should be submitted in advance to allow our team enough time to prepare the required products.

Special Occasion Orders

We prepare baked products for birthdays, baby showers, graduations, anniversaries and other celebrations.

Customers can contact us to discuss their requirements and create an order suitable for their occasion.
